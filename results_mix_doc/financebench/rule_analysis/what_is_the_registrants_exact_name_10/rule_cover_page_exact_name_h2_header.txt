Root cause: H2 is not a consistent structural level for the company name.

The rule looks exclusively for H2-level section_header spans on page 1 that match a
legal-entity suffix (inc., corp., corporation, company, plc). It only fires for eBay
because eBay's cover page happens to label "eBay Inc." as an H2 node. In every other
filing the company name is either:
  - An H1 span (most 10-K/10-Q cover pages)
  - A text or section_header node at a different level
  - Not labelled with a legal suffix in the span text (e.g. "3M COMPANY" passes but
    most company names in this set do)

The H2 slots in other documents contain boilerplate like "ANNUAL REPORT PURSUANT TO
SECTION 13", state/EIN fields, address lines, or form period text — none of which are
company names. The rule's H2 assumption is overfitted to a minority document layout.

Fix direction: Change the level filter from H2-only to H1-or-H2, or drop the level
filter entirely and rely on the legal-suffix pattern plus page-1 + section_header
constraints, which is what the higher-accuracy rules do.
