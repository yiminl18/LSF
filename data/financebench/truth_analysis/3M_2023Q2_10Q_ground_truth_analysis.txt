Q23 — Total Revenue for Most Recent Fiscal Year
------------------------------------------------------------

Evidence [p3 income statement table]:

  (Three months ended / Six months ended June 30, 2023 / 2022)

Note: $16,356M is the six-month (H1 2023) net sales figure, not a full fiscal year, and the
financial statements are explicitly labeled "Unaudited." The question asks for the "most recent
fiscal year from the audited income statement," which technically does not exist in a 10-Q.
The GT benchmark interprets "most recent fiscal year" as the filing period (H1 2023).

Verdict: AMBIGUOUS. The $16,356M figure exists in the document. The model was correct that
it is unaudited and not a full-year figure, but wrong to say the data is absent from the filing.
GT is applying a loose interpretation of "fiscal year."
