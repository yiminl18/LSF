(Jovernment

Itivolves merely

In the same bank.

In these accounts proceeds

to public debt
securities entered for
their own account

their

The tax and loan account system permits the Treasury
to leave funds
In banks
and
In
the
oom-uunltles

the funds
for Its

In
this way

Is
able
to neutralize
the
