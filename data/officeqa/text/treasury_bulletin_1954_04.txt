ana

9'*-0,

e.xpendltures

Daily Cash Statement

In addition.
It will

Monthly Budget Statement
The new monthly

accurate

and expenditures

the United
States Government

plus or deficit.

of

lections

t.ie various agencies,

This change

' s budget.
