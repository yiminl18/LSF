refunded in full by four new 91-day issues.

to $1.6 billion, the equivalent of that which matured. The average 
rates of discount on the new issues were 2.562 percent for June 7» 
2.531 percent for June 14-; 2.4-30 percent for June 21; and 2 .535 
percent for June 28.
