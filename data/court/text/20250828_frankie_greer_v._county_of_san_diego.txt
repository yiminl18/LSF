ORDER
Judge Graber and Judge Callahan voted to deny the
petition for panel rehearing. Judge Koh voted to grant the
petition for panel rehearing. Judge Callahan voted to deny
the petition for rehearing en banc, and Judge Graber so
recommended. Judge Koh voted to grant the petition for
rehearing en banc.
The full court was advised of the petition for rehearing
en banc. A judge of the court requested a vote on whether
to rehear the matter en banc. The matter failed to receive a
majority of votes of the nonrecused active judges in favor of
en banc consideration. Fed. R. App. P. 40. Judge Owens
did not participate in the deliberations or vote in this case.
The petition for panel rehearing and rehearing en banc,
Dkt. No. 76, is DENIED.
