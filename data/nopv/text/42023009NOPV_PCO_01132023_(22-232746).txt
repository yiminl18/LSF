From March 21 through November 22, 2022, a representative of the Pipeline and Hazardous
Materials Safety Administration (PHMSA) pursuant to Chapter 601 of 49 United States Code
(U.S.C.) inspected UCAR Pipeline Inc.’s (UCAR) gaseous ethylene system in Texas and
Louisiana.
As a result of the inspection, it is alleged that you have committed a probable violation of the
Pipeline Safety Regulations, Title 49, Code of Federal Regulations (CFR). The item inspected and
the probable violation is:
1.

§ 192.616 Public Awareness
(a) . . . .
(c) The operator must follow the general program
recommendations, including baseline and supplemental requirements
of API RP 1162, unless the operator provides justification in its
program or procedural manual as to why compliance with all or certain
provisions of the recommended practice is not practicable and not
necessary for safety.

UCAR failed to follow the general program recommendations, including baseline and
supplemental requirements of API RP 1162 in accordance with § 192.616(c) and its procedures.
Specifically, UCAR failed to conduct a program effectiveness evaluation every four years as
required by Section 8.4 of API RP 1162 and its procedures. 1 While UCAR provided annual
reviews from 2018 to 2021, section 8.4 of API RP 1162 and its procedures specifically require a
program effectiveness evaluation every four years, in addition to annual reviews.
Therefore, UCAR failed to follow the general program recommendations, including baseline and
supplemental requirements of API RP 1162 in accordance with § 192.616(c) and its procedures.

PROPOSED COMPLIANCE ORDER
Pursuant to 49 U.S.C. § 60118, the Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration
(PHMSA) proposes to issue to UCAR Pipeline Incorporated a Compliance Order incorporating
the following remedial requirements to ensure the compliance of UCAR Pipeline Incorporated
with the pipeline safety regulations:
A.

With regard to Item 1 of the Notice pertaining to UCAR’s failure to conduct a
program effectiveness evaluation, UCAR must conduct a program effectiveness
evaluation and provide the evaluation to the Director, Southwest Region within 90
days of issuance of the Final Order.

B.

It is requested (not mandated) that UCAR Pipeline Incorporated maintain
documentation of the safety improvement costs associated with fulfilling this
Compliance Order and submit the total to Bryan Lethcoe, Director, Southwest
Region, Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration. It is requested
that these costs be reported in two categories: 1) total cost associated with
preparation/revision of plans, procedures, studies and analyses, and 2) total cost
associated with replacements, additions and other changes to pipeline
infrastructure.
