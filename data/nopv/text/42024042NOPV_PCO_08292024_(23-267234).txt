From April 17 to 19 and on May 10, 2023 of the on-site inspection, a representative of the Pipeline
and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration (PHMSA), Office of Pipeline Safety (OPS),
pursuant to Chapter 601 of 49 United States Code (U.S.C.) inspected your Maxville liquefied
natural gas (LNG) facility in Jacksonville, FL.
As a result of the inspection, it is alleged that you have committed probable violation of the
Pipeline Safety Regulations, Title 49, Code of Federal Regulations (CFR). The item inspected and
the probable violation is:
1.

§ 193.2637 Remedial measures.
Prompt corrective or remedial action must be taken whenever an
operator learns by inspection or otherwise that atmospheric, external,
or internal corrosion is not controlled as required by this subpart.

Eagle LNG Partners failed to take prompt corrective or remedial action whenever an operator
learns by inspection or otherwise that atmospheric, external, or internal corrosion is not controlled
as required by this Part 193, Subpart G in accordance with § 193.2637. Specifically, during the
inspection, the PHMSA inspector observed extensive outer corrosion on the N2 Recycle
Compressor especially near the flanges.

PROPOSED COMPLIANCE ORDER
Pursuant to 49 United States Code § 60118, the Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety
Administration (PHMSA) proposes to issue to Eagle LNG a Compliance Order incorporating the
following remedial requirements to ensure the compliance of Eagle LNG with the pipeline safety
regulations:
A.

In regard to 1 of the Notice pertaining to Eagle LNG’s failure to take prompt
corrective or remedial action whenever an operator learns by inspection or
otherwise that atmospheric, external, or internal corrosion is not controlled as
required by this Part 193, Subpart G, Eagle LNG must reinspect the N2 Recycle
Compressor, remediate all corrosion, and provide inspection records and
photographs of the remediation of the N2 Recycle Compressor to the Director,
Southwest Region, PHMSA within 60 days of receipt of the Final Order.

B.

It is requested (not mandated) that Eagle LNG maintain documentation of the safety
improvement costs associated with fulfilling this Compliance Order and submit the
total to Bryan Lethcoe, Director, Southwest Region, Pipeline and Hazardous
Materials Safety Administration. It is requested that these costs be reported in two
categories: 1) total cost associated with preparation/revision of plans, procedures,
studies and analyses, and 2) total cost associated with replacements, additions and
other changes to pipeline infrastructure.
